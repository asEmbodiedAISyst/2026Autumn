## Available material

Original phone receiver, fake-phone input, pusher and gripper teleoperation scripts, model assets and recorded example.

## Evidence and execution boundary

The core practice is iPhone teleoperation of the simulated pusher to push a cube. The fake phone checks an input/mapping path. Physical operation and gripper comparison are optional. Verify package-specific phone axes before interpreting directions.

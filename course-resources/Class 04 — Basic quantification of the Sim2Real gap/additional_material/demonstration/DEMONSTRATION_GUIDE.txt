XC330 MATCHED HARDWARE AND MUJOCO DEMONSTRATION
================================================

Episode:
B1__H2__P06_heldout_composite__R01

Condition:
B1 fixture, 22 g link and two complete 44 g payload-and-pin assemblies at -80 mm and +80 mm. Controller H2 uses raw P/I/D registers 800/0/150.

asbrain_log/
command_events.parquet contains the external command and recorded inner position/velocity references with write-request timestamps.
telemetry_events.parquet contains the measured actuator response with receive timestamps.
episode.json records the episode identity, conditions and file contract.

asmagic_media/
The MP4 and before-motion photograph carry the same episode identifier and provide visual context for the motor-side record.

hardware_runner/
collect_b1.py is the exact B1 collection entrypoint. Its inherited collection modules, bus interface, profile generator, media helper, base configuration and B1 overlay are kept beside it so the bounded live-run logic can be inspected without reconstructing imports.

Corresponding replay:
paper/figures/heldout_demo_episode.png shows the external command, recorded inner reference, hardware response, synthetic baseline, hardware-fitted replay and residuals.

Checks:

- initialize once from the first measured active position and velocity;
- do not inject measured state during replay;
- interpret the phone record as context, not position ground truth;
- keep the B1 load, H2 controller and episode timestamps attached to the result.

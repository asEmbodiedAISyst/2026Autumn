PREPARED DIGITIZATION DEMONSTRATION
===================================

RS06 reference
- Open rs06/RS06_vendor_motor.step in Fusion.
- Compare the imported geometry with rs06/RS06_outline_and_mounting_dimensions.png.
- Inspect component origin, output axis, mounting interfaces and Fusion physical properties.
- Treat Fusion mass and inertia as CAD estimates. The manual mass is a manufacturer value, not a measurement of the available unit.
- The current package contains the motor STEP, not a complete jointed RS06 mechanism or an RS06 MJCF export.

Recorded Fusion and ACDC4Robot sequence
- fusion_acdc/Fusion_revolute_joint_preview.png: inspect the joint type and axis.
- fusion_acdc/Fusion_physical_material.png: verify the material used for CAD properties.
- fusion_acdc/Fusion_rigid_payload_joint.png: distinguish rigid attachments from the revolute joint.
- fusion_acdc/Fusion_scripts_and_addins.png and ACDC4Robot_selected.png: load the add-in.
- fusion_acdc/ACDC4Robot_toolbar.png and ACDC4Robot_MJCF_export.png: select MJCF and MuJoCo export.
- These screenshots record the XC330 workflow. Repeat the checks on the RS06 assembly before describing an RS06 export.

MuJoCo checks
- Confirm that mesh paths resolve.
- Confirm the output-axis direction and reference pose.
- Confirm declared joint limits.
- Compare gravity-on and gravity-off behavior.
- Keep visual geometry separate from simplified collision geometry.

H2D setup
- Import h2d/Link_for_H2D.3mf in millimetres.
- Select Bambu Lab H2D, the installed 0.4 mm nozzle, the installed plate and the labelled PLA preset.
- Lay the broad face on the plate, auto-arrange, slice and inspect the preview.
- Show the prepared printed link and printer setup; the demonstration does not wait for printing to finish.

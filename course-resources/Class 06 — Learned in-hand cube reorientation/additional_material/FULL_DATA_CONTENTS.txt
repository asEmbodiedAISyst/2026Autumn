CONTINUOUS POSITIVE-Z ROTATION EVIDENCE
=======================================

Active scope
------------
The active report documents only the continuous proprioceptive rotation policy.
It is neither visual servoing nor goal-conditioned cube reorientation.  Camera
RGB is retained solely as documentary evidence and is never supplied to the
actor.

Reproducible evidence
---------------------
evidence/videos/continuous_z_model400_preview_30s.mp4
  Supplied 30-second simulation preview of model_400.

evidence/videos/rgb.mp4
  Recent iPhone RGB recording of guarded physical execution.

evidence/simulation_frames/ and evidence/hardware_frames/
  The twenty uniformly sampled source frames used in the paper sequences.

photos/contact_sheet.png
  Six marked faces of the 58 mm / 85 g MBZUAI cube, used as the paper's
  Appendix C object-reference figure. It is not visual feedback to the actor.

results/fig06_simulation_continuous_z_sequence.png
results/fig07_hardware_continuous_z_sequence.png
  Publication-ready filmstrips.  Simulation and hardware are intentionally
  separate figures.

results/fig08_measured_60s_safety_telemetry.png
results/fig09_measured_joint_tracking.png
results/fig10_deployment_summary.png
  Reproducible plots generated from actual bridge telemetry. They report motor
  behavior and safety quantities, not cube orientation.

results/fig11_training_diagnostics_job16352.png
  Supplied PPO diagnostics for the continuous proprioceptive-rotation job.
  They show an early, non-converged policy and are retained as diagnostic,
  rather than rotation-success, evidence. The separate visual_yaw_metrics.png
  is excluded because it belongs to a visual-yaw task outside active scope.

code/run_continuous_z_with_iphone_recording.sh
  The only supported deployment launcher.  It requires --iphone-ip because the
  address may change, records RGB, and calls the guarded bundle deployment.

code/generate_rotation_sequences.py
  Deterministically rebuilds both filmstrips from the archived source videos.

The checkpoint, checksum, training YAML, hardware bridge, and NPZ telemetry
remain in CONTINUOUS_Z_ROTATION_DEPLOYMENT_BUNDLE_20261014.  This prevents
duplicate executable code and keeps the deployment artifact authoritative.

# 21 Final Project Showcase

The **2026 Autumn showcase** brings all eight teams together for live presentations, questions, posters and awards. We adapt [RSS's live presentation over video](https://roboticsconference.org/2026/information/presentationInstructions/) to our class: **each team's presentation is followed immediately by its own Q&A**, with the other teams in the audience.

## Session programme

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 09:00–09:10 | Set up posters and check video playback. |
| 09:10–10:10 | **Team01–Team08**, in order: four-minute presentation, three-minute Q&A and 30 seconds to change teams. |
| 10:10–10:40 | Poster session: all eight posters open for discussion, with Audience Choice voting. |
| 10:40–10:50 | Awards session. |

## Team presentations and Q&A

All members of the presenting team come to the front. A team speaker presents live while the team's video plays in the background. Keep the talk within four minutes and rehearse with the video's fixed timing.

Use the video already submitted for Class 20, with its recorded soundtrack muted during playback. Explain the project live; existing captions can remain visible. No second video file or additional slide deck is required.

**Immediately after the talk, the whole presenting team stays at the front for three minutes of Q&A.** The other seven teams remain in the audience and ask questions, alongside the teaching team. The instructor moderates; relevant team members answer. The next team then takes over. Continue longer technical discussions at the posters.

## Poster session

All teams then present their submitted posters at the assigned positions in L12. Keep at least one member at each poster and rotate roles so others can visit projects. All members should remain available for questions from the teaching team.

Explain your contribution, main result and important limitation. Use your submitted repository, results or video for detail. Distinguish your evidence from published examples and identify changes since the collected submission.

If operating physical hardware, use the tested configuration and confirm the operator, stop and reset arrangements with the instructor/TA.

## Awards

The final ten minutes celebrate the projects and presentations:

| Award | Recognises |
| --- | --- |
| **Best Overall Project** | The strongest combination of contribution, execution and evidence. |
| **Reality Check Award** | The clearest evidence of what works, what fails and where the claim stops. |
| **Ingenious Engineering Award** | A creative, effective solution using the available hardware or software. |
| **Best Research Communication** | The clearest live explanation, poster and answers to questions. |
| **Audience Choice** | The project students would most like to explore further. |

The teaching team selects the first four awards. For Audience Choice, each student votes once during the poster session for a team other than their own. These awards recognise achievement and do not change course grades.

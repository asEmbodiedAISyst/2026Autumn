# 21 Final Project Showcase

The **2026 Autumn showcase** brings all eight teams together for live presentations, questions, posters and awards. We adapt [RSS's live presentation over video](https://roboticsconference.org/2026/information/presentationInstructions/) to our class: **each team's presentation is followed immediately by its own Q&A**, with the other teams in the audience.

Follow the project scope and demonstration plan agreed with the instructor under the [course assessment guidance](student.md#project-scope-and-assessment-guidance). The video-backed presentation is required for every team. Conditional instructions for operating hardware below are operating arrangements, not approval of a simulation-only assessment scope or a new requirement for every team to run hardware live. Live demonstrations and awards do not replace the assessed code, results and video.

## Session programme

All times are Abu Dhabi time (UTC+4). The showcase takes place in Classroom 6 (CR6).

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10:30–10:34 | Opening and final playback check |
| 10:34–11:10 | **Team01–Team08**, in order. Each team has 3 minutes to present, 1 minute for Q&A and 30 seconds to switch. |
| 11:10–11:24 | Posters and live demonstrations in parallel |
| 11:24–11:28 | Awards |
| 11:28–11:30 | Wrap-up and cleanup |

## Team presentations and Q&A

All members of the presenting team come to the front. A team speaker has three minutes to present live. Rehearse a three-minute excerpt from the submitted video and know where to start playback.

Use the video already submitted for Class 20, with its recorded soundtrack muted during playback. Explain the project live; existing captions can remain visible. No second video file or additional slide deck is required.

**Immediately after the talk, the whole presenting team stays at the front for one minute of Q&A.** The other seven teams remain in the audience and ask questions, alongside the teaching team. The instructor moderates; relevant team members answer. Allow 30 seconds for the next team to take over. Continue longer technical discussions at the posters and demonstrations.

## Poster session

All teams then present their submitted posters at their assigned stations in CR6, alongside any live demonstrations. Keep at least one member at each poster and rotate roles so others can visit projects. All members should remain available for questions from the teaching team.

Explain your contribution, main result and important limitation. Use your submitted repository, results or video for detail. Distinguish your evidence from published examples and identify changes since the collected submission.

If operating physical hardware, use the tested configuration and confirm the operator, stop and reset arrangements with the instructor/TA.

## Awards

The four-minute awards session celebrates the projects and presentations.

| Award | Recognises |
| --- | --- |
| **Best Embodied AI Project Award** | The strongest integration of embodiment, experience and intelligent behaviour, supported by evidence. |
| **Best Experience Generation Award** | The most effective generation of useful experience for learning or evaluation. |
| **Best Live Demo Award** | The clearest, most convincing live demonstration of a working system. |
| **Best Poster Award** | The clearest visual explanation of the project, results and contribution. |
| **Best Presentation Award** | The clearest live presentation and answers to questions. |
| **Best Reality Check Award** | The clearest evidence of what works, what fails and where the claim stops. |

The teaching team selects these awards. Live demonstrations can take place during the poster session. These awards recognise achievement and do not change course grades.

## Arrive ready

Prepare your poster and demonstration station before 10:30. The teaching team preloads the eight submitted videos on one laptop. Rehearse your three-minute excerpt with muted playback and bring a device that can open your repository and results.

Use the artifacts already submitted under [Poster submission](module-05-final-project-submissions/18-final-poster-submission.md#submission-requirements), [Class 19 — Code](module-05-final-project-submissions/19-final-code-submission.md) and [Class 20 — Video](module-05-final-project-submissions/20-final-video-submission.md). **Class 21 adds no new submission.** Check the [course deadlines](student.md#important-deadlines) for the submission times.

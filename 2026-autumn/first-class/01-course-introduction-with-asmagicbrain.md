# 01 Course Introduction with asMagicBrain

#### Purpose

Introduce the course's physical–digital experimentation pathway, eight-team organization, source repositories, evidence standards, and final project.

#### Learning objectives

* Distinguish a motor from an integrated actuator, its sensing, drive electronics, transmission, and controller.
* Distinguish a command, an internal reference, and a measured response.
* Read model-specific actuator specifications and operating limits.

#### Lecture — 40 minutes

1. Course questions; embodiment and controlled action; the six-stage project record; demonstration versus student simulation; expectations for reproducibility and attribution.

#### Break — 5 minutes

#### Instructor/TA demonstration — 25 minutes

The published course and project repositories, a prepared physical platform, MuJoCo, and a sample dataset. asMagicBrain may be shown as the instructor’s authoring tool; students can use GitHub/GitBook directly.

#### Student activity — 10 minutes

Formation of 8 student teams

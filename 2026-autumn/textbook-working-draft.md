## Interpreting the examples

Constructed calculations and simulation results support the stated mathematical and implementation questions under their declared assumptions. They do not establish commercial actuator fidelity, physical transfer or general policy reliability. Chapter 7 distinguishes a separate Gaussian uncertainty fixture from robot observations; Chapter 13 identifies the trajectory subsets used for its prediction comparison. Appendix C identifies the records and verification limits.

The textbook is supporting reading, not an additional submission. Return to the [course home and teaching schedule](student.md#teaching-schedule).

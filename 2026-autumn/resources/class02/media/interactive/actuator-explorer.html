Explore one mechanism at a time. These deterministic mathematical illustrations are not MuJoCo simulations or measured actuator responses. Use the recorded figures in the lesson to test physical claims.

What changes when the demanded effort reaches its limit?

XC330 H1 modelling map: demand = 1.579836 × position error + 0.202115 × velocity error. Limited effort is clipped to ±0.1551 N m. This is the disclosed engineering map, not an independent torque calibration.

Predict first: leave velocity error at 0.20 and change position error from 0.05 to 0.10. Does applied effort double? Return position error to zero: why can effort remain nonzero?

How many observations describe the rise?

Constructed response: 30 × (1 − exp(−t/0.03)) degrees. The grey curve is known only because this example defines it; a physical logger supplies the blue samples.

Predict first: change 16 ms to 32 ms. Has the mechanism changed, or only the observation? Can more samples recover an unknown command-transmission delay?

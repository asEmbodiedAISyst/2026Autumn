SIX-STAGE RECORD
================

Introduction
Defines the XC330, asBrain Box, asMagic capture, software and safety boundary.

Claim2Design
Defines the fixture configurations, controller conditions, trajectories, repetitions and expected comparisons before hardware evaluation.

Design2Data
Checks the simulation episode contract, generated trajectories, model loading and synthetic identifiability.

Data2Model
Fits candidate effective models on calibration data and uses separate model-selection profiles to choose one candidate per controller.

Model2Prototype
Preserves the physical fixture record, exact execution settings, raw asBrain logs, matched asMagic media, episode list and operator note.

Sim2Real
Freezes the selected candidates, opens held-out data, replays the recorded inner trajectories and reports paired position error.

Rules used here
---------------
- The hardware controller, experiment runner and simulated actuator remain separate components.
- Episode identity links commands, telemetry, fixture, controller, phone media and replay.
- Calibration estimates parameters; model-selection chooses a candidate; held-out data tests the frozen choice.
- The primary metric is equal-weight mean per-episode active-profile position RMSE in radians.
- Poor-agreement cases are retained. No failed accepted episode was present in the five source campaigns.
- M0 has no external fixture. B0/B1/B2/G1 each use a measured 22 g link and two measured 44 g complete payload-and-pin assemblies, for 110 g total.
- Results are valid only for the stated one-actuator conditions.

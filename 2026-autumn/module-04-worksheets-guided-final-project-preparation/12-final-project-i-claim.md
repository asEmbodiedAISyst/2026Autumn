# 12 Final Project I: Claim

#### Project question

What capability are we testing, and under which conditions?

#### Three components

* **Worked example — 10 minutes:** A LEAP-based object-retention and rotation study is narrower than reproducing every manipulation, accessibility, and durability claim in the LEAP paper. The hardware name does not identify the contribution.
* **Worksheet review — 15 minutes:** summarize prepared evidence on Worksheet 1 — Claim; challenge an assumption or missing observation.
* **Project studio — 85 minutes:** Choose one capability from your project idea. Write two competing claim sentences, then select the one you can challenge with the available time and equipment. Record why the other is too broad. Specify a baseline and one pilot trial, keeping the outcome criterion unchanged after you observe the result.

#### Checkpoint

Commit the claim, its proposed protocol, baseline, and resource check. A numerical result is not required at this first checkpoint.

# 17 Final Project VI: Decision

#### Project question

What conclusion and next action does the evidence justify?

#### Three components

* **Worked example — 10 minutes:** For a simulation-only manipulation project, the next action might be release of the simulation package. Hardware deployment remains unsupported. Checks outside that decision require a reasoned NA; missing evidence needed for the decision remains NR.
* **Worksheet review — 15 minutes:** summarize prepared evidence on Worksheet 6 — Decision; challenge an assumption or missing observation.
* **Project studio — 55 minutes:** Prepare evidence references before class. Independently inspect consequential checks, reconcile disagreements, then decide the bounded next action. Use the full two-sided screen. A completed screen is not a readiness award, certification or grade calculated by averaging.

#### Checkpoint

Commit the completed screen, supported final claim, conditions and reproducibility instructions at the evidence freeze. Keep subsequent changes traceable.

# 08 Let's Build a Simple Arm & Turn it On

#### Central question

What evidence shows that an arm or its simulation is ready for a controlled experiment?

#### Lecture — 40 minutes

Reuse actuator and model concepts from Classes 02–07. Introduce the reBot RS joint/gripper arrangement, frames, limits, calibration and commissioning gates. Separate assembly, communication readiness and motion readiness.

#### Break — 5 minutes

#### Demonstration — 25 minutes

Use the reBot RS MuJoCo model as the current course baseline. Inspect the configuration, joint order and feedback, then demonstrate bounded movement. When physical arms are available, teaching staff preassemble them; students may complete a few final screws/connections and the supervised commissioning checklist. A properly operating arm is the preferred physical-session outcome.

#### Student activity — 10 minutes

Inspect the simulation's joint order, limits and reference configuration. Complete a short commissioning checklist. No reBot/LeBot interchangeability is planned.

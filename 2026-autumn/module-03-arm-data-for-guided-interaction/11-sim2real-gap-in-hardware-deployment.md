# 11 Sim2Real Gap in Hardware Deployment

#### Central question

What evidence supports a claim that a learned behavior transfers and remains useful?

#### Lecture — 40 minutes

1. Observation/action compatibility, normalization, joint order and calibration.
2. Timing, latency, actuation and contact mismatch.
3. Offline replay → shadow checks → supervised physical rollout when hardware is available.
4. Nominal versus held-out conditions, failures and bounded claims.
5. Connect actuator/payload validation with LEAP and reBot task-level evaluation.

#### Break — 5 minutes

#### Demonstration — 25 minutes

Use prepared deployment checks, successful/failed rollouts and aligned measurements. If physical deployment is not ready, explicitly label the demonstration simulation-only and identify the missing physical evidence.

#### Student activity — 10 minutes

Diagnose one supplied failure, propose the next experiment, and draft a project claim with its evidence requirements.

#### Project handoff

Provide the six-stage record template and repositories. Unfinished printing, identification, data collection, retargeting or training may continue during project work time. A simulation/iPhone project is the viable baseline when additional hardware is unavailable.
